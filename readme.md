# 📚 AI Study Buddy: Chat, Summarize & Quiz

AI Study Buddy is a Streamlit-based web application designed to help students learn faster. Upload your PDFs or images of handwritten notes, and the AI will generate concise summaries and interactive practice quizzes to test your knowledge.

## ✨ Features
* *📄 PDF Support:* Upload textbooks or lecture slides.
* *🖼️ Image OCR:* Upload photos of your notes (PNG/JPG) for instant digitizing.
* *📝 Smart Summarization:* Condense long chapters into key bullet points.
* *🧠 Quiz Generation:* Automatically generate practice questions based on your specific materials.
* *💬 Contextual Chat:* Ask follow-up questions about your data in a ChatGPT-like interface.
